## 🎨 Color Coding

- **Header (नमुना-२)**: Red (#d32f2f) - High visibility
- **Section Headers**: Blue (#1e88e5) - Professional
- **Subtotals**: Green (#4caf50) - Money amounts
- **Grand Total**: Red border - Emphasis
- **Instructions**: Yellow highlight (#fff9c4) - Important info
- **Info Sections**: Light gray (#f5f5f5) - Background

## 📐 Layout Specifications

- **Page Size**: A4 (210mm × 297mm)
- **Margins**: 10mm all sides
- **Border**: 3px double border
- **Font**: Noto Sans Devanagari (Marathi), Arial (English)
- **Base Font Size**: 11pt
- **Line Height**: 1.5

## ✨ Key Features Visible in Layout

1. **Prominent Form Number**: नमुना-२ at top
2. **Clear Instructions**: 30-day deadline mentioned
3. **Bilingual**: All text in Marathi and English
4. **Three-Part Structure**: Self, Party, Others expenses
5. **Grand Total**: Clearly highlighted
6. **Legal Limit**: Reference provided
7. **Declaration**: Bilingual legal statement
8. **Signatures**: Candidate and Officer sections
9. **Footer**: Document instructions
10. **Timestamp**: Generation date/time
