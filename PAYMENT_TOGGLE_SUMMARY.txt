🔄 HOW TO SWITCH MODES:

Method 1: Admin Panel (Recommended)
────────────────────────────────────
Visit: /admin/payment-settings.jsp
Click on desired mode → Save

Method 2: Direct SQL
────────────────────
For QR Code:
  UPDATE system_settings SET setting_value='qrcode' 
  WHERE setting_key='payment_mode';

For Razorpay:
  UPDATE system_settings SET setting_value='razorpay' 
  WHERE setting_key='payment_mode';
